The story

One crown.
Four nations.

Look closely at the King of Britain crest: a lion, and a shield carrying England, Scotland, Wales and Ireland. That is the idea behind the race. One weekend, one course, and one crew leaves as King of Britain.

The format comes from the desert. In 2007 a small group of teams lined up in Johnson Valley, California, for the first King of the Hammers: a race that mixed flat-out desert running with brutal rock crawling. Out of it grew the Ultra4 racing series, and from that, Ultra4 Europe, where independent organisers run the "King of" rounds across the continent.

King of Britain brings that format to UK soil. It tests three things at once: how fast the car is, how well the driver reads the line, and how well the team prepares. A stuck car is not out. Crews winch, rig and drag themselves up the rocks. Spectators watch it happen from a few metres away.

In 2027 King of Britain opens the European season. The first points of the year are won in Britain.

How Ultra4 racing works

01

Speed

Fast sections where the car has to fly: gravel, ruts, jumps and long straights.

02

Rock

Technical rock sections where line choice, spotting and the winch matter more than horsepower.

03

Endurance

Lap after lap against the clock. Cars break, crews fix them, and the race goes on.

04

Safety

Scrutineering, marshals on every section and full safety equipment for every crew.

Pick your weapon

Classes

King of Britain runs on Ultra4 Europe classes. The final class list and technical rules for 2027 are set in the event regulations.

4400

Unlimited

The top class. Purpose-built race cars with very few limits on engine, suspension or chassis. The fastest machines in the field.

  • Fastest
  • Purpose-built
4500

Modified

Production-based engine and drivetrain with tighter limits on tyres and suspension. Close racing on smaller budgets.

  • Production-based
  • Limited tyres
4600

Stock

Vehicles that stay close to factory spec, with the original frame and body. The way in for many first-time Ultra4 crews.

  • Factory frame
  • Entry level
4800

Legends

Old-school solid-axle cars with limited suspension travel. Hard on the driver, loved by the crowd.

  • Solid axle
  • Old school
UTV

UTV / SSV

Side-by-side vehicles built from production UTVs. Light, quick and growing fast across Europe.

  • Side-by-side
  • Production

Class summaries are a guide only. The technical regulations for King of Britain 2027 are the binding rules.

Questions

FAQ

When and where is King of Britain 2027?

Provisionally 19–21 March 2027 in England. The exact venue will be confirmed and published on this page.

What is Ultra4 Europe?

The European championship of Ultra4 racing, which began with King of the Hammers in the USA. It mixes high-speed off-road racing with extreme rock sections. In 2027 it has five rounds: Britain, Nations, France, Poland and Portugal.

Which classes can race?

Ultra4 Europe classes: 4400 Unlimited, 4500 Modified, 4600 Stock, 4800 Legends and UTV/SSV. The final list is set in the event regulations.

How do I enter?

Online registration opens soon. Register your interest now through the contact form and we'll send you the entry form and regulations as soon as they are published.

Can I come and watch?

Yes. Ultra4 is made for spectators: you can watch from a few metres away behind the tape. Ticket and parking details will be published with the venue.

How can I volunteer?

Choose "Volunteer" in the contact form. We need marshals, recovery and paddock helpers.
